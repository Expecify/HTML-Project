Northfield Surgery

Welcome to Northfield Surgery, a fictional medical practice created as a student project. Our goal is to provide a user-friendly platform for booking appointments and accessing information about our services and clinicians.

How to Book an Appointment

Booking an appointment at Northfield Surgery is simple and convenient. Follow these steps to schedule your visit:

  1. Navigate to the "Book an appointment" page.
  2. Select a date and time that works for you.
  3. Choose a clinician from the available options.
  4. Provide your contact information and any relevant details about your visit.
  5. Review your appointment details and confirm.

Opening Hours

Our surgery is open during the following hours:

  • Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

Urgent Help

If you require urgent medical assistance, please contact your local emergency services (at 999) or visit the nearest hospital. For non-urgent matters, you can reach out to our surgery during opening hours.
