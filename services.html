Our Services

At Northfield Surgery, we offer a range of medical services to meet the needs of our patients. Our team of experienced clinicians is dedicated to providing high-quality care in a friendly and welcoming environment.

General Practice

Our general practice services include routine check-ups, diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses, management of chronic conditions, and preventive care. We are committed to helping you maintain your health and well-being.

Specialist Services

In addition to general practice, we offer specialist services in areas such as dermatology, cardiology, and mental health. Our specialists work closely with our general practitioners to provide comprehensive care for our patients.

Health Screenings

We provide a variety of health screenings to help detect potential health issues early. Our screenings include blood pressure checks, cholesterol tests, diabetes screenings, and more. Early detection can lead to better outcomes and improved quality of life.

Vaccinations

We offer a range of vaccinations to protect against various diseases. Our vaccination services include flu shots, travel vaccinations, and routine immunizations for children and adults. Our team will provide you with the necessary information and guidance to ensure you receive the appropriate vaccinations for your needs.
